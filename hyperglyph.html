🧩 Hyperglyph Manuscript

Shannon's slot-language reader. The words that look like words aren't words — they're hyperglyphs: little rebus-pictures made of slot-syllables that point somewhere, like "see page 17, figure 4." Only the blue words are links now — click a whole word and the view jumps to the folio it points to, and reads the rebus there: one emoji per slot, plus what the word is predicted to mean. The slots themselves aren't clicked; they work behind the scenes to build the reading.
