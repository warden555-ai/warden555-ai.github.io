The Voynich Comparison

The closest real book to the Voynich manuscript: the Tractatus de Herbis — an illustrated herbal from southern Italy, around 1300 (the Egerton 747 copy sits in the British Library).

Picture of the plant on top, the plant's name in big letters, the remedies below it — written as orders: take this, do that. It has alphabetical finding tables, and later hands added notes and corrections over the years. An apothecary's quick-reference — a book you check, not a book you read.

Same job Shannon's slots do. The picture pages are the quick reference, the text is the shorthand. The difference: the Tractatus is written in Latin. The Voynich got its own script.
